# College PYQ Repository

A community-maintained collection of college previous-year question papers, built to help students find useful study material in one place.

## About

The College PYQ Repository is intended to make previous-year question papers easier to discover and access. It is a shared resource for students across semesters and subjects, maintained by the student community so it can remain useful beyond any one batch or graduating class.

## What you'll find here

- Previous-year question papers organised by subject, semester, and academic year
- Search and filtering to help students find relevant papers
- A community contribution process for submitting papers
- Review and moderation to help keep the public collection organised and reliable

## Community contributions

Students are encouraged to help improve the collection by sharing papers they are permitted to distribute. Submitted material should be accurate, clearly labelled, and relevant to the course.

Submissions may be reviewed before they appear in the public repository. Please do not upload private information, answer scripts containing personal details, or material you do not have permission to share.

## Project status

The website is being developed in stages. Some parts of the interface may currently use demonstration data, and submission, review, or file-storage features may not yet be connected to a live backend. The project will be updated as these features become available.

## Built for continuity

This is a community resource, not a personal archive. The goal is to keep it useful as students graduate and new batches take over. Clear documentation, responsible moderation, and shared maintenance are important to its long-term future.

## Disclaimer

This repository is an independent student resource and is not an official college publication unless explicitly stated. Always follow your college's academic policies and respect copyright and distribution restrictions when sharing documents.
